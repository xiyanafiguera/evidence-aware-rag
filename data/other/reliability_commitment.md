# Our Reliability Commitment

Document ID: MKT-001
Published: March 2026 (northstar.example/reliability)

At Northstar Cloud, reliability is a product feature. Our engineering team targets 99.99% uptime
across all regions, with multi-zone redundancy and continuous monitoring.

When things go wrong, we communicate transparently on our status page and make it right with our
customers.

*This page describes our engineering goals. Contractual commitments and service credits are defined
in the Service Level Agreement.*
