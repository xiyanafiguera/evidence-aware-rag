# Northstar Platform — Service Level Agreement (v3)

Document ID: SLA-003
Effective: October 1, 2026 until further notice
Supersedes: SLA-002

## Scope

Applies to Business and Enterprise plans. Starter plans are not covered. Individual contracts may
define different terms; where they do, the contract prevails.

## Availability commitment

Monthly availability of at least 99.9% for the Northstar Platform API and dashboard, measured per
calendar month and excluding announced maintenance windows.

## Service credits

| Monthly availability | Service credit (% of monthly fee) |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Below 99.9% and at or above 99.5% | 15% |
| Below 99.5% | 25% |

## Claim process

Credits are no longer applied automatically. The customer must submit a credit request to
support within 30 days after the end of the affected calendar month. Requests received after that
period are not eligible.

## Exclusions

Credits do not apply to downtime caused by customer actions, force majeure events, or third-party
services outside Northstar Cloud's control.
