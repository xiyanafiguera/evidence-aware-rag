# Refund Policy (v1)

Document ID: REF-001
Effective: January 1, 2026 to August 31, 2026

## Monthly subscriptions

Customers on a monthly subscription may cancel and receive a full refund of their first payment if
they cancel within 30 days of that first payment. After 30 days, payments are non-refundable and
cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.

## Annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are non-refundable. Customers may cancel renewal at any time before the
renewal date.

## How to request a refund

Refund requests are submitted to billing@northstar.example through the account owner's email.
