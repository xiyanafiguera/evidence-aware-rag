# Northstar Platform — Service Level Agreement (v1)

Document ID: SLA-001
Effective: January 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026

## Scope

This Service Level Agreement applies to customers on the Business and Enterprise plans of
Northstar Platform. Starter plans are not covered.

## Availability commitment

Northstar Cloud commits to a monthly availability of at least 99.5% for the Northstar Platform API
and dashboard. Monthly availability is calculated as the percentage of minutes in a calendar month
during which the service was available, excluding announced maintenance windows.

## Service credits

If monthly availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a service credit
calculated as a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected month:

| Monthly availability | Service credit |
|----------------------|----------------|
| Below 99.5% and at or above 99.0% | 5% |
| Below 99.0% | 10% |

## Claim process

Service credits are applied automatically to the next invoice. No request from the customer is
required.

## Exclusions

Credits do not apply to downtime caused by customer actions, force majeure events, or third-party
services outside Northstar Cloud's control.
