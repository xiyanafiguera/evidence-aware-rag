# DRAFT — Service Level Agreement v3 (proposal)

Document ID: DRF-001
Status: Draft for leadership review — not in force
Prepared: September 10, 2026, by Customer Success

## Motivation

Following the August reliability issues, Customer Success proposes a more generous credit scheme
to protect enterprise relationships.

## Proposed service credits

| Monthly availability | Proposed credit (% of monthly fee) |
|----------------------|------------------------------------|
| Below 99.9% and at or above 99.5% | 20% |
| Below 99.5% | 30% |

## Proposed claim process

Customers would request credits within 45 days after the end of the affected month.

## Open questions

- Financial impact of the higher percentages has not been estimated.
- Whether to keep automatic credits for Enterprise customers.

This proposal is to be discussed at the leadership meeting on September 20, 2026.
