# Customer Support Policy

Document ID: SUP-001
Effective: January 1, 2026

## Support tiers

| Plan | Channels | First response target |
|------|----------|-----------------------|
| Starter | Email | Next business day |
| Business | Email and chat | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Email, chat and phone, 24/7 | 1 hour for Sev-1, 4 hours for Sev-2 |

## Service credits

Starter plans are excluded from service credits under the Service Level Agreement, regardless of
availability in a given month. Business and Enterprise customers are covered by the Service Level
Agreement in force, unless their contract states otherwise.

## Severity definitions

- Sev-1: platform unavailable or data pipelines stopped for most users.
- Sev-2: major feature degraded, workaround available.
- Sev-3: minor issue or question.
