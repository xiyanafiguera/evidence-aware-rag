# Refund Policy (v2)

Document ID: REF-002
Effective: September 1, 2026 until further notice
Supersedes: REF-001

## Monthly subscriptions

No change from the previous version: a full refund of the first payment is available if the
customer cancels within 30 days of that first payment. Later payments are non-refundable.

## Annual subscriptions

New in this version: customers who cancel an annual subscription within the first 90 days receive
a prorated refund for the unused months. After 90 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable.

## How to request a refund

Refund requests are submitted to billing@northstar.example through the account owner's email.
