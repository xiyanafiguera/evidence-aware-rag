# Northstar Platform — Service Level Agreement (v2)

Document ID: SLA-002
Effective: July 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026
Supersedes: SLA-001

## Scope

Applies to Business and Enterprise plans. Starter plans are not covered.

## Availability commitment

Northstar Cloud commits to a monthly availability of at least 99.9% for the Northstar Platform API
and dashboard (previously 99.5%). Monthly availability is the percentage of minutes in a calendar
month during which the service was available, excluding announced maintenance windows.

## Service credits

| Monthly availability | Service credit (% of monthly fee) |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Below 99.9% and at or above 99.5% | 10% |
| Below 99.5% | 20% |

## Claim process

Service credits are applied automatically to the next invoice.

## Exclusions

Credits do not apply to downtime caused by customer actions, force majeure events, or third-party
services outside Northstar Cloud's control.
